## ✦ HYPERDEITY — She Can Do Anything

A playable 3D hyperpop altar. A chrome goddess floats above a mirrored stage,
wearing a crown of shards and a holographic gown. Command her and the world
changes around her.

### Powers

| Command | Key | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Create Life** | `1` | Souls are born in her hand and take up orbit around the altar. |
| **Destroy** | `2` | Every soul implodes, the camera shakes, the world glitches, and the light drains out. |
| **Change** | `3` | Reality recompiles into a new palette: Bubblegum, Acid Bloom, Cryo Dream, or Solar Flare. |
| **Everything** | `4` / `Space` | All of it at once: a supernova, a new realm, and a screen-wide revelation. |

### Controls

- **Drag** to orbit the altar, **scroll / pinch** to zoom.
- **1–4** or **Space** on a keyboard.
- **Sound** starts on the first command and can be toggled from the top bar.

### Tech

- Three.js `r170`, vendored locally so the experience runs without a network.
- Custom chrome / iridescent goddess built from lathed and extruded geometry.
- Mirrored floor, orbital rings, floating Y2K trinkets, soul particles, shockwaves,
  sparks, bloom, film grain, chromatic aberration, and a glitch grade.
- Web Audio ambient pad and synthesized sound design.
- Responsive from 390 px phones to widescreen desktops, with touch and safe-area support.

The static site is served from the repository root; no build step is required.
