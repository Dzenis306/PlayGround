## IRONBLOOM — Big Lifts. Small Boss.

Mia is 12, and she has just bought the keys to a dusty neighborhood gym. You have **10 days** to train clients, keep the floor spotless, pay rent, and turn the place into the strongest little gym in town.

### Play

Open `index.html` through any static server, or run:

```bash
./start.sh
```

The game is dependency-free HTML, CSS, and Canvas 2D. Progress saves automatically to `localStorage` (`ironbloom-save-v1`), and the title screen offers **Continue** after a reload.

### How to play

- **Coach a client:** pick a client from the front desk, match the session focus to their goal for a 30% pay bump, and choose Steady, Focused, or Strict intensity.
- **Five-rep mini-game:** press **Space**, click the form bar, or tap **Lock in form** when the needle is inside the form window. Perfect reps build the most reputation.
- **Run the floor:** spend action points to clean, tune up equipment, or post flyers for walk-ins.
- **Renovate:** install permanent upgrades and hire staff. Wages and rent are deducted when you close up.
- **Reach five stars by day 10.** Strict form is the fastest path, but the window is narrow.

### Controls

| Action | Desktop | Mobile |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lock in a rep | `Space` / click | Tap the form button or bar |
| Close a dialog | `Esc` / close button | Tap the close button |
| Coach / care / shop | Click | Tap |

The layout adapts from wide desktop screens down to a 390px phone viewport, with a sticky header and scrollable front desk.
